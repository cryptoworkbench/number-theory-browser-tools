<objective>
Add the geometric half of the tool: a rectangle-tiling view beside the numeric chain that shows each division step as "cut the largest square you can out of this rectangle, repeatedly", kept in lockstep with the chain's current line, and hard-capped at 40 drawn squares so a 250,000-tile quotient renders in a fraction of a second instead of freezing the tab.

Purpose: the numeric chain proves the algorithm; the rectangle proves *why* it works — the last rectangle tiles exactly, and the side of that square is the GCD. This is the plan where the phase's one genuine performance hazard lives, so the cap is designed in from the first line of the render function rather than bolted on after a freeze (`02-RESEARCH.md` Pitfall 1, carried in STATE.md as this phase's standing blocker). The cap doubles as a legibility floor: because at most 41 columns are ever drawn across a fixed-width viewBox, every square is guaranteed at least ~17 px wide.

Output: an `svgEl` helper, a `TILE_CAP` constant, a `#tileSvg` panel with its caption and cap note, and a current-step link that makes the chain and the diagram two views of one cursor.

Wave rationale: wave 3, after 02-02. Every expansion plan in this phase edits the same single-file tool, so same-wave parallelism is impossible — the plans run one wave apiece. This one comes after the presets because it is verified against them: `500000, 2` is the cap's acceptance input, `144, 12` is the perfect-tiling case, and `17, 0` is the no-rectangle case, and all three are one click away only once plan 02-02 has landed.
</objective>
