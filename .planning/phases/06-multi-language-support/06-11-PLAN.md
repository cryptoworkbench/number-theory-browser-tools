<objective>
Translate the RSA walkthrough — by far the largest body of prose on the site (≈17,500 characters of English) — completely into all five languages in three passes: static narrative, key generation and wire, then Eve and the encrypted correspondence.

Purpose: RSA renders whole sections from template-literal innerHTML strings and holds user state (keys, typed messages, CRT toggles, Eve's results) that a language switch must not destroy; building each prose node with bindText makes it re-render on its own while inputs, listeners and results stay in place.
Output: the converted page, its data file, optional gate config.
</objective>
