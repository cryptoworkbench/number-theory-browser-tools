<objective>
Make the Venn Diagram tool's right-hand "Region composites" pane double-clickable: an overlap chip becomes a door into the tool that independently confirms what that overlap claims — the Euclidean Algorithm for any two-way overlap (both modes), the Chinese Remainder Theorem for the three-way overlap (three-circle mode only).

Purpose: the composites pane already prints the answer each region implies, but the learner has to take it on faith. A two-way overlap says "these two numbers share exactly this much"; the Euclidean Algorithm proves it by division. The three-way overlap's composite says "this is the number every one of these three circles carries inside it"; the CRT proves that number can be rebuilt from its residues alone. Right now reaching either proof means retyping numbers by hand.

Output: two edited files. `Venn Diagram/venn-diagram.html` gains two URL builders, a `currentTriple()` total-deriver, a link-capable composite chip, and a new-tab opener. `Chinese Remainder Theorem/chinese-remainder-theorem.html` gains inbound seeding params — the one thing it lacks today, since its existing cross-link to the Euclidean page is outbound only.
</objective>
