## Decisions Made

See `key-decisions` in frontmatter. Primary decision: the CRT tool's seed moduli must be a fixed pairwise-coprime basis table independent of the `abc` region's own primes, because those primes divide all three circle totals by construction and would force every CRT remainder to zero — the tool would technically be correct (0 ≡ composite mod composite) but would visually display 0, defeating the demonstration's purpose. The basis table is walked in ascending-span order; the first basis whose span strictly exceeds the composite is used, keeping the CRT's rendered strip as short as possible while staying inside that tool's own `MAX_MODULUS`/`MAX_SPAN`/`MAX_COUNT` guards by construction.
