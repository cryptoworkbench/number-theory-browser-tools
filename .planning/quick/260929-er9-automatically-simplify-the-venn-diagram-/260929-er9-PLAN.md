<objective>
Make the Venn Diagram tool self-simplifying: after every edit, a prime is redrawn in the single region that correctly names which circles it divides, instead of being duplicated across regions that contradict each other.

Purpose: today the tool lets a user assert two incompatible facts at once — 5 in `A \ B` says "5 divides A but not B", 5 in `B \ A` says "5 divides B but not A" — and the page then prints `A ∩ B = 30 ∩ 35 = 5` in prose while its own lens chip reads 1. The diagram stops teaching the moment it disagrees with itself. Reactive canonicalization makes the picture the answer.

Output: one new mode-agnostic canonicalizer plus a check-then-commit wrapper in `Venn Diagram/venn-diagram.html`, wired into all six mutators and both restore paths; no new files, no new dependencies.
</objective>
