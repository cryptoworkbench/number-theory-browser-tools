<objective>
When a user hovers a Venn Diagram overlap region's composite chip, show a live miniature of the Euclidean Algorithm tool's nested-squares diagram for that region's two numbers — alongside, not instead of, the deep link that is already there.

Purpose: the overlap chip already says "this is the GCD, double-click to go confirm it by repeated division", but the user has to leave the page to see what that confirmation looks like. A thumbnail of the nesting turns the cross-link into a promise the page can keep in place: you see the two circles' numbers collapse into one rectangle of gcd-sized squares while still looking at the Venn diagram that produced them.

Output: one new self-contained section in `Venn Diagram/venn-diagram.html` (Euclidean step walk + nested layout + SVG preview renderer + hover/focus wiring), plus one preview `<g>` layer per composite SVG. No new files, no new dependencies.

**Decision — approach:** port the math (background option 1), not an iframe (option 2). An iframe of `euclidean-algorithm.html` would drag in that page's nav header, controls, playback engine and captions just to show one 900x420 SVG, cannot be cropped to that SVG without reaching into the framed document, and would re-fetch and re-boot a 1195-line page on every hover. The pieces actually needed are small and pure: a ~10-line Euclidean step loop and the ~40-line `computeNestedLayout()`. Duplicating them is exactly what CLAUDE.md already mandates for number-theory helpers across these single-file tools.

**Decision — alongside vs. instead:** the SVG preview panel REPLACES the native `<title>` tooltip on linked chips only, and its own caption plus the `double-click to open the full view` hint carry that tooltip's information. Rationale: two tooltips triggered by one hover fight over the same pixels — the browser's `<title>` bubble would pop up on top of the panel a half-second later saying the same thing — so the panel absorbs the tooltip rather than sitting next to it, while the deep link itself (the `data-xref-href` + double-click handler + `.xref` prose anchor) is untouched. The tooltip's exact text moves verbatim to `aria-label`, so nothing is lost to a screen reader.
</objective>
