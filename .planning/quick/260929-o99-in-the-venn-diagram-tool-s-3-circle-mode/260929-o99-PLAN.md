<objective>
In the Venn Diagram tool's three-circle mode, make the hover thumbnail also appear on the triple-overlap (A∩B∩C) centre chip, matching the hover behaviour the pairwise overlap chips already have.

Purpose: the centre region is the one region in three-circle mode whose number is the most interesting (it *is* gcd(A, B, C)), and it is the only overlap region a user cannot preview. Today the preview is wired only to chips that also carry a Euclidean Algorithm deep link, and the centre chip carries none — so "has a link" and "has a preview" have to be decoupled before the centre chip can preview.

Output: one edited file, `Venn Diagram/venn-diagram.html`, plus a scratchpad-only headless-Chrome behavioural harness (not committed).
</objective>
