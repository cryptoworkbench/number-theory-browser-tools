<objective>
Replace the Venn Diagram tool's global "Hover: Euclid squares" / "Hover: factor tree" toolbar toggle with a single combined hover-preview panel that stacks both visualizations and lets the user scroll down to see the second one.

Purpose: today the two miniatures are mutually exclusive and swapping between them costs a trip to the toolbar, so a user comparing "what is the GCD run" against "what are the factors" can only ever see half the story at a time. Stacking both into one scrollable panel removes the mode concept entirely — one hover, both views.

Output: one edited file, `Venn Diagram/venn-diagram.html`, plus a scratchpad-only headless-Chrome behavioural harness (not committed).
</objective>

<source_plan>
The approved implementation plan lives at `/home/mainaccount/.claude/plans/let-s-discuss-this-regards-woolly-rivest.md` and is the source of truth for scope. Read it once before Task 1. This PLAN.md is that plan turned into executable tasks, with two grounded additions the approved plan did not have visibility into:

1. **The wheel listener cannot live on the panel.** The approved plan says "attach a `wheel` listener on the panel". That cannot fire: `.nested-preview{ pointer-events:none; }` (`venn-diagram.html:310`) is deliberate and load-bearing — its own code comment at `:306-309` explains that a panel which swallowed the pointer would fire `mouseleave` on the chip and flicker the preview out of existence. Additionally the panel is offset from the chip by `NP_GAP`(26), so the cursor is over the chip, not the panel, for the whole hover. The listener therefore goes on the chip `<g>` in `appendCompositeBadge()`, alongside the `mouseenter`/`mouseleave`/`focus`/`blur` listeners already there. Do NOT relax `pointer-events:none`.
2. **The keyboard gap gets closed, not just flagged.** The approved plan's last verification bullet flags "wheel-scroll won't work via keyboard" as a limitation to surface rather than silently ship. Task 2 discharges it with four lines of ArrowDown/ArrowUp handling on the already-focusable chip, rather than shipping a section only mouse users can reach.

Everything else follows the approved plan as written, including its explicit non-goals: no region gains a preview it does not already have, no new deep link is introduced anywhere, and the Factor Tree section gets no click hint.
</source_plan>
