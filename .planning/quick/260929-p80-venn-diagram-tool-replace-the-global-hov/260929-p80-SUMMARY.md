# Quick Task 260929-p80: Combined stacked hover-preview panel Summary

**Replaced the Venn Diagram tool's mutually-exclusive "Hover: Euclid squares" / "Hover: factor tree" toolbar toggle with one fixed 268x196 panel that stacks both miniatures and lets wheel/ArrowDown/ArrowUp scroll between them.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** 17 min
- **Started:** 2026-09-29T16:21:08Z
- **Completed:** 2026-09-29T16:38:05Z
- **Tasks:** 3
- **Files modified:** 1

## Accomplishments
- One hover now always shows BOTH the Euclidean nested-squares view and the Balanced (Fermat's Method) factor tree, stacked inside a single clipped, fixed-footprint panel — no toolbar mode decides which one renders.
- Wheel and ArrowDown/ArrowUp scroll the panel between the two sections, clamped at both ends, and reset to the top on every fresh hover or keyboard focus; a top-right "more" affordance marks that a second section sits below and disappears once scrolled past it.
- The now-redundant Hover-preview toolbar group, its `previewMode` state field, its `venn-diagram-preview` localStorage key, its reader/setter, its two click listeners and its init-time call are all deleted outright — a fresh browser profile never writes that key again.
- The approved plan's own flagged limitation ("wheel-scroll won't work via keyboard") was closed in Task 2, not shipped as a known gap: ArrowDown/ArrowUp on a focused chip scroll the same panel with the same clamps.

## Files Created/Modified
- `Venn Diagram/venn-diagram.html` - Combined `showRegionPreview()` panel builder, `drawEuclidSection()`/`drawTreeSection()` section renderers, `scrollNestedPreview()` scroll/affordance helper, wheel+keydown listeners on the chip `<g>`, two new token-only CSS rules (`.np-heading`, `.np-scroll-hint`), and full removal of the previewMode toolbar toggle and its state

## Decisions Made
- Both section renderers now draw a caption even when their own content bails (empty/degenerate Euclidean run; out-of-range factor-tree value), per the plan's explicit instruction, so one section's failure can never blank the section stacked next to it. This is a deliberate behavior change from the pre-change renderers, which drew nothing at all on bail.
- The scroll offset is stored both as a `data-offset` attribute on the scroll `<g>` and encoded in its `transform` translate, giving the harness (and any future maintainer) a direct, unambiguous read of the logical scroll position rather than having to parse the transform string's sign convention.
- Wheel and keydown listeners were wired onto the chip `<g>`, per the plan's `<source_plan>` correction to the originally-approved design — `.nested-preview{ pointer-events:none; }` is load-bearing (documented in the file's own comment at the CSS rule) and the panel sits `NP_GAP` away from the chip, so the cursor is over the chip for the whole hover, never the panel.

## Deviations from Plan

None - plan executed exactly as written. (The two `<source_plan>` corrections listed above — wheel listener on the chip, not the panel; keyboard gap closed rather than flagged — were already part of the plan's own instructions, not deviations discovered during execution.)
