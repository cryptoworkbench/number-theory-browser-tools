- **Q-05 Register.** All three use the informal second-person singular, matching nl je / de du / es tú / it tu / pl ty / pt-PT tu / sv du / nb du.
  - ro **tu/tău**: apasă, alege, fă clic, scrie, introdu, vezi, încearcă, trage, mută.

- **Q-06 Orthography and punctuation.**
  - ro: comma-below ș ț Ș Ț (U+0219, U+021B, U+0218, U+021A), never the cedilla forms U+015F/U+0163; â/î per the 1993 norm (sunt, când, până, România). Quotes „…” (U+201E … U+201D).
  - hu: double-acute ő ű Ő Ű (U+0151, U+0171), never õ û ô. Quotes „…” (U+201E … U+201D). Ordinals are written with a period after the number (`{index}. lépés`).
  - lv: modern orthography (ā ē ī ū č ģ ķ ļ ņ š ž), never the pre-1946 ŗ or ō. Quotes „…“ (U+201E … U+201C). Ordinals are written with a period (`{index}. solis`).
  - All three: no space before `:` `;` `?` `!`; headings and tool names in sentence case like fr/es/it/pl/pt/sv/nb; a value containing a straight ASCII apostrophe must be double-quoted, like the French nav values in site.js.
- **Q-07 Plurals.** CLDR 48 (node 22.23.1 / ICU 78.2, verified at planning time). Each plural value carries exactly the categories Intl.PluralRules reports, in CLDR order (zero, one, two, few, many, other), and every form carries English `other`'s placeholders (English `one` and `other` share placeholders in all 11 entries).
  - ro `{ one, few, other }`: one = exactly 1; few = 0, 2–19, 101–119, 201–219 … and every fractional count (1.5); other = 20–100, 120–200 … and 1000000. Noun form: one singular (`1 pas`), few bare plural (`2 pași`, `19 pași`), other with `de` (`20 de pași`).
  - hu `{ one, other }`: one = exactly 1. Hungarian nouns stay singular after a numeral, so `one` and `other` are usually identical; they differ only where another word refers back to a plural (euclid.nestedNoteCapped: `ennél a lépésnél` / `ezeknél a lépéseknél`). NEW-PLURAL requires at least 1 hu value with differing forms.
  - lv `{ zero, one, other }`: zero = 0, 10–20, every count ending in 0 or in 11–19 (including 1000000); one = 1, 21, 31 … 101 (ending in 1 but not 11); other = 2–9, 22–29 … and fractional counts (1.5). Noun form: zero genitive plural (`10 soļu`), one nominative singular (`21 solis`), other nominative plural (`2 soļi`). Words that agree with the noun must agree in every form; prefer invariant verbs (first-person plural, `ir`) or a label form.
  - NEW-PLURAL requires at least 3 ro values and 3 lv values whose forms are all distinct.
  - Numbers that are not the entry's own count, and every number inside a plain-string value: ro and lv phrase them so no surrounding word depends on the number's value — a label/colon form (ro `Căsuțe create: {n}`, lv `Izveidoti lodziņi: {n}`), a symbol, or an attributive form valid for every count (lv `{n} bitu`). This is the Polish rule in glossary (a). Hungarian needs no such rephrasing for noun number.
  - `dh.bitOption` (options 16/32/64): ro `biți: {n}`, hu `{n} bites`, lv `{n} bitu`. Never `{n}-bit` (identical to English).
- **Q-08 Grammar around placeholders and narrative names.**
  - hu: never attach to a placeholder a suffix whose form depends on how the substituted value is pronounced (-nak/-nek, -val/-vel, -ban/-ben, -ra/-re, -t, -szor/-szer/-ször, -adik/-edik). Allowed: an invariant suffix after a hyphen (`{n}-ig`, `{n}-kor`, `{n}-ként`), the ordinal period, a postposition (szerint, után, alatt, között, mellett, számára, által, felett) or a label/colon form. Before a placeholder write the article as `a(z)` or omit it where Hungarian allows.
  - hu names: never attach a suffix to Alice, Bob or Eve, literal or placeholder; any suffix goes on the possessed noun instead. Use the possessed-noun construction (`Alice kulcspárja`, `🔑 {0} kulcspárjának generálása`, `📢 {0} nyilvános kulcsa`) or a postposition (`Bob számára`, `Eve által`).
  - ro names: genitive/dative with `lui` + the invariant name (`lui Alice`, `lui Bob`, `lui Eve`, `lui {0}`); the English placeholder possessive `{0}'s X` becomes `X … a lui {0}` (`🔑 Generează perechea de chei a lui {0}`, `📢 Cheia publică a lui {0}`).
  - lv names: Alice, Bob and Eve stay invariant (the page script passes the English names into `{0}`). A name in an oblique role is introduced by an apposition noun that carries the case: `puse` for Alice/Bob (`🔑 Ģenerēt puses {0} atslēgu pāri`, `📢 Puses {0} publiskā atslēga`, `pusei Bob`), `uzbrucēja` for Eve (`uzbrucējai Eve`). A subject in the nominative uses the bare name (`Alice aprēķina …`, `Eve uzvar.`).
