# Quick 261003-i23: Drop the 240px footer reserve on RSA/DH; lift the pinned panel above the footer instead

User evidence ("NEW RSA webtool screenshot.png", "NEW Diffie-Hellman webtool screenshot.png", 2736x1823):
after 261003-fcr the footer is flush, but on RSA and Diffie-Hellman it is a ~300px tall band, mostly
empty, because the 240px panel reserve is the footer's own padding-bottom.

## Task 1 — remove the reserve, lift the panel

- Delete `.site-footer{ padding-bottom: 240px; }` (and its comments) from both pages, so the footer is
  the same height as on every other page.
- Panel `bottom` becomes `calc(clamp(8px, 2vw, 16px) + var(--footer-lift, 0px))`.
- Page-local `liftAboveFooter()` sets `--footer-lift` on the panel to the part of the site footer that
  is inside the viewport (`max(0, innerHeight - footer.top)`), run on scroll/resize via a rAF-throttled
  scheduler (RSA reuses its existing one), so at the end of the page the panel rests just above the
  footer and never covers the language switcher.
- Reveal logic is unchanged (RSA reveal is viewport-relative and needs no scroll-past room).
