<objective>
Add a third view, "Circle composites", to the Venn Diagram in both two-circle and three-circle mode. It is a read-only SVG that draws the circles completely separated (no overlap). Each circle holds every prime that lies anywhere in that circle in the interactive diagram, plus that circle's composite number (the product of those primes). It sits beside the existing "Interactive diagram" and "Region composites" panes, refreshes whenever they do, and is translated into all sixteen languages.

Purpose: Region composites shows the product of each region. Circle composites shows the number each whole circle stands for: A, B (and C) as composites assembled from their primes, with the shared primes visibly counted in every circle that contains them. That makes "A ∩ B = gcd" readable at a glance.
Output: updated `Venn Diagram/venn-diagram.html`, three new keys × 16 languages in `assets/i18n/venn-diagram.js`, and a headless-Chrome probe `circle-probe.js` in this quick-task directory.
