<objective>
Add a second, normally folded part to the Venn Diagram's two-circle mode that shows why lcm(A, B) = (A × B) ÷ gcd(A, B). It uses the A and B currently placed in the two-circle diagram.

The idea it teaches: A's primes are A\B plus A∩B, and B's primes are A∩B plus B\A. So A × B contains the A∩B primes twice. Dividing by gcd(A, B), the product of A∩B, removes one copy. What is left is exactly the union of the Venn diagram, and its product is lcm(A, B).

The panel shows this in three animated stages with step controls. Under the diagram is an exact BigInt equation line, e.g. lcm(12, 18) = (12 × 18) ÷ 6 = 216 ÷ 6 = 36. Every word is translated into all sixteen languages.

Purpose: a self-learner who has just placed primes into A\B, A∩B and B\A can watch the shared primes get double-counted and then divided out once. That turns the formula into something seen rather than memorised.
Output: an updated `Venn Diagram/venn-diagram.html`, 12 new keys × 16 languages in `assets/i18n/venn-diagram.js`, a headless probe `lcm-probe.js`, and the day/night screenshots in this quick directory.

Deviations from the orchestrator's design direction (each one forced by the codebase; record them in the SUMMARY):
1. The notation is lowercase `lcm(A, B)` / `gcd(A, B)`, not LCM/GCD. This page already prints literal `gcd(a, b)` in its previews, and the dictionary header says that notation never changes. The ru/el script rule in i18n-check.js only allow-lists lowercase `gcd`/`lcm`, so uppercase notation inside a translated caption would fail SCRIPT-LATIN.
2. `assets/nt-bigint.js` is NOT included and `fmt` is not used. Native `BigInt` is enough for exactness. `fmt`'s thousands commas would also collide with the comma between function arguments (lcm(1,000,003, 999,983) is unreadable), and every other numeral on this page is printed ungrouped. So the include list and the import block stay unchanged.
3. The fold is not persisted: it is collapsed on every load. That matches "normally hidden/folded" and adds no new storage key.
4. The fold sits after `#message`, outside `#frame-two`, so the diagram's status line stays right under the diagram. `setMode()` hides it in three-circle mode.
</objective>
