## Locked decisions (orchestrator; non-negotiable)

- L-ZH: Chinese means Simplified Chinese (Mandarin, mainland standard), code zh, switcher label 中文. Browser detection resolves every Chinese tag (zh, zh-CN, zh-SG, zh-Hans, zh-TW, zh-HK, zh-Hant, zh-Hant-TW, any case, - or _) to zh, so Traditional-script readers get Simplified rather than English. The code allow-list stays exact and case-sensitive on every other channel (zh-CN, zh-Hans, ZH rejected by setLang, ?lang= and storage).
- L-JAKO: Japanese ja with label 日本語, Korean ko with label 한국어. Order after Kiswahili: 中文, 日本語, 한국어 (SUPPORTED_LANGS, SWITCHER_OPTIONS, every data file).
- L-LTR: all three are left to right; none joins RTL_LANGS (engine or checker); no isolate, bidi mark or embedding in any zh/ja/ko value.
- L-PLURAL: {other} is the only plural category for all three (Intl.PluralRules confirms ['other'] in this repo's Node 22.23.1); the engine and checker must accept a one-category plural object.
- L-SCRIPT: zh values in Han (CJK Unified Ideographs) plus CJK punctuation; ja values in Han, Hiragana and Katakana (including the prolonged sound mark ー); ko values in Hangul (no hanja). Notation allow-list as for the other non-Latin languages: mod, gcd/lcm where kept literal, single-letter variables, acronyms (RSA ...), code identifiers, Alice/Bob/Eve (chosen here: kept in Latin script, exactly as ru/el/he/hi/ar keep them, so SCRIPT_LATIN_NOTATION is reused unchanged). zh and ja have no spaces between words, so the checker tokenises by script runs, never by whitespace; Korean uses spaces. Cross-language guards: zh rejects kana and Hangul; ja rejects Hangul (and Simplified-only forms, nice-to-have, implemented here via HAN-FORM); ko rejects kana and Han.
- L-DIGITS: ASCII digits only. Banned: full-width digits U+FF10-U+FF19, full-width Latin letters and ASCII variants in formulas, CJK numerals used where the English has digits, and 万/億/만/억 grouping. zh, ja and ko join DIGIT_PARITY_LANGS. Full-width CJK punctuation (，。：？（）「」) is fine in prose; formulas inside values keep ASCII operators and parentheses.
- L-FONT: system fallback font, as for ru/el/he/hi/ar: no font link, @font-face or font-family change. No CSS may mangle CJK (uppercase transform, letter-spacing, ::first-letter, italic); a :root[lang="zh"|"ja"|"ko"] rule is added only for a real rendering issue (precedent: Arabic's letter-spacing reset). Line breaking at 375px is checked; word-break: keep-all for Korean only if needed. (Both measured during planning and found needed: D-CJK-EM and D-KO-BREAK below.)
- L-SOURCE: English is the source of truth; translate every zh, ja and ko value from its en entry. Never derive one CJK language from another (in particular never adapt a zh value into ja or the reverse); every key of every namespace gets a value in all three; no key is skipped, shortened or left English.

- D-SWITCHER: in each page header, directly after the line carrying the Kiswahili option, three lines with the same indentation: `<option value="zh" lang="zh">中文</option>`, `<option value="ja" lang="ja">日本語</option>`, `<option value="ko" lang="ko">한국어</option>` (no dir or selected attribute). - D-REGISTER-zh: second person 你 (when a pronoun is needed at all); instructions as plain imperatives (点击、选择、输入、拖动、按、查看、试试); buttons as verbs or verb-object phrases (播放、暂停、重置、生成、清空、添加、撤销、重做); validation states the problem (请输入……, ……必须是……); status banners in the perfective (已找到……, 已完成).
- D-REGISTER-ja: です・ます style for prose and messages; instructions 〜してください (クリックしてください、選択してください、入力してください、ドラッグしてください、押してください、試してください); buttons as nouns (再生、一時停止、リセット、生成、クリア、追加); validation …を入力してください / …である必要があります; status …が見つかりました / 完了しました. No あなた.
- D-REGISTER-ko: 합니다체 for prose, messages and status (…합니다, …했습니다); instructions in the polite -세요 form (클릭하세요、선택하세요、입력하세요、드래그하세요、누르세요、시도해 보세요); buttons as nouns (재생, 일시 정지, 초기화, 생성, 지우기, 추가); validation …해야 합니다. No 당신.
- D-PUNCT: zh prose uses full-width ，。：；？！（） and the enumeration comma 、, quotes “…” (U+201C/U+201D), ellipsis …… (two U+2026), dash —— (two U+2014, no spaces) for an English " — ". ja prose uses 、 and 。, full-width ：？！（）, quotes 「…」 (nested 『…』), ellipsis ……, dash ——. In zh and ja an ASCII , . ; : ? ! never directly follows a Han or kana character (CJK-ASCII-PUNCT). ko uses Western punctuation exactly like English (. , ? ! : ; ( ) and " — " with spaces, quotes “…”, ellipsis …) and never a full-width or CJK punctuation mark (FULLWIDTH-FORM). In all three: formulas, numerals, coordinate/argument tuples and a parenthetical whose content is only Latin letters, digits, placeholders or operators keep ASCII punctuation and ASCII parentheses (FULLWIDTH-FORMULA); the playback glyphs and legend slots ({0} Prime, ▶ Play) keep their space as in English; a value whose English has no closing punctuation gets none.
- D-SPACING: zh puts exactly one ASCII space between a Han character and an adjacent Latin or Greek letter, ASCII digit or {placeholder}, on both sides (使用 RSA 加密, 第 {index} 步, 欧拉 φ 函数, 2048 位) and none next to full-width punctuation (ZH-SPACING). ja puts no space between a Japanese character (kanji, kana, ー) and an adjacent Latin or Greek letter, digit or placeholder (RSAの公開鍵, {count}個, オイラーのφ関数) (JA-SPACING); spaces between two Latin tokens and around operators inside formulas stay as in English in both. ko follows standard Korean word spacing; particles, the copula and counters attach directly to the preceding word, Latin token, digit or placeholder (RSA를, 12단계, {count}개).
- D-PARTICLES-ko: never attach a sound-dependent particle (은/는, 이/가, 을/를, 와/과, (으)로) directly to a placeholder, whose final sound is unknown (KO-PARTICLE); restructure with a noun after the placeholder that carries the particle ({n} 값을, {count}단계를) or use an invariant particle (의, 에, 에서, 에게, 도, 만, 까지, 부터, 보다); the dual form 을(를) is not used. After a literal Latin token choose the particle by its Korean reading: Alice (앨리스), Eve (이브), RSA, DH, ECDH, CRT, QFT and the letters p q e d g a b x y k G A B take 가/는/를/와/로; Bob (밥) and the letters n m N r l take 이/은/을/과/으로 (n = 엔, m = 엠, r = 알, l = 엘).
- D-COUNTERS: there is no plural inflection, so the single {other} form must read correctly for 0, 1 and any count. zh: numeral + classifier + noun ({count} 个素数, {count} 步, {n} 位, {count} 次, {n} 毫秒). ja: {count}個の素数 or 素数{count}個, {count}ステップ, {n}ビット, {count}回, {n}ミリ秒. ko: 소수 {count}개, {count}단계, {n}비트, {count}번, {n}밀리초. D-PLURAL-FORMS: every plural key is written as {other: "..."} only, keeping every placeholder of the English other form; the English one form's wording is not copied into a separate key.
- D-NUMWORDS: where English has digits, the CJK value has exactly those digits (DIGIT-PARITY), never a CJK numeral in their place (Step 3 → 第 3 步 / ステップ3 / 3단계). Where English writes a quantity in words without digits, the CJK value uses words or native numerals, never added digits: a billion times → 十亿次 / 十億回 / 십억 번; hundreds of digits → 数百位 / 数百桁 / 수백 자리; thousands of nodes → 数千个节点 / 数千個のノード / 수천 개의 노드; binary → 二进制 / 二進 / 이진 (never 2진). The English "1 trillion" (factorTree.msgTooLargeClassic, venn.msg.tooLarge) is zh 1 万亿, ja 1兆, ko 1조: the only scale word after a digit (CJK-MYRIAD allows it only where English has a digit followed by million, billion or trillion).
- D-HANFORM: zh uses Simplified forms only and ja uses Japanese shinjitai only. The checker's HAN_FORM_FORBIDDEN lists the characters most likely to slip in from the wrong standard, written exactly as follows. zh (Traditional forms and Japanese shinjitai whose Simplified form differs): 們這個時對說為進設計鑰碼圖樹質無從選擇顯輸結鍵實現點擊開關變層類單給經錯誤運發應構換環輪線圓題頁兩幾費際間門問認識證書長東車見觀規則數與會來號條歐餘裡後図実関発対説択単変経歩剰円両応証読転続検 . ja (Simplified-only forms whose shinjitai differs): 们这个时对说为进设计钥码图树质无从选择显输结键实现击开关变层类单给经错误运剩发乘应构换环轮线圆题页步两几费际间门问认识证书长东车见观规则么吗 .
