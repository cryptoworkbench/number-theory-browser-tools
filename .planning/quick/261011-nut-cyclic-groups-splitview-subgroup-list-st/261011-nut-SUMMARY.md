# Phase quick-261011-nut Plan 01: Cyclic Groups Splitview Summary

Splitview on the Ring window's "Subgroup visualized" row stacks the live "Subgroup generated by each element" panel under the ring behind a horizontal divider; the desktop's Subgroups window steps aside while split, and Fit is split-aware.

## What was built

**Task 1 (b8807b0)** - the end-to-end toggle.
- `#split-btn` ends `#subgroup-panel` and `#ring-split` is the last child of `.diagram-frame`. Both are in the ring spec's `ids`, and `rows` gives the button `'gen'`, so `partHolder` makes it the last part of `.fs-win-row-gen`. Extra ring frames get both through `enterRingWin`.
- CSS: the button and pane are `display:none` outside a Ring window body. The pane has a `border-top` divider and no margin. `.fs-win-body.is-ring:has(> .ring-split:not([hidden]))` adds a final `minmax(0, 1fr)` row and a `split` area, so the ring row and the pane share the spare height. All colors are `var()` tokens.
- Move, not clone: `syncSplit()` moves `#subgroups-list-panel` into the pane and leaves a comment placeholder (`splitHome`). Closing the split, or leaving full screen, sends it back. `syncWindows()` calls it after mounting the windows and before the task-button loop. A placeholder cut loose by leaving full screen is dropped (`isConnected` guard).
- `splitView: 'Splitview'` is in the `en` block only and in the header comment's English-only paragraph.
- `split-probe.js` with S1-S8.

**Task 2 (574bf89)** - the desktop.
- `hasContent` is false for a panel whose first part sits in the pane. Its task button and menu item are disabled, and Tile/Cascade/frontKey skip it. CSS hides a panel window whose `.fs-win-body` is `:empty`.
- Fit: `RING_ROW` 4 -> 5 and a new `SPLIT_ROW = 7`. `ringNeeds` returns `flex` (2 while split, else 1). `fitRing` takes `side = min(bodyW - padX, (D.H - extraH - chromeH) / flex)` and sizes the window to `side * flex + chromeH + extraH`.
- The frame's measure message carries `flex`. The desktop accepts a measure message only when `d.flex === 1 || d.flex === 2` (T-nut-01).
- Probe grows to S1-S12.
