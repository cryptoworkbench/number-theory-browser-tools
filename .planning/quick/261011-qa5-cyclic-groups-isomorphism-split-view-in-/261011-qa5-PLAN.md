<objective>
Open an isomorphic group beside the current ring, in the same view. Clicking an isomorphism chip no longer opens a new Ring window (commit 122f203) or a new tab. It splits the view: the current group stays on the left, and the chip's group appears in a right half with its own per-group controls. Settings the two rings share are shown once, on the left, and drive both. The two rings turn together and highlight each other's corresponding beads, mapped through the isomorphism g^k ↔ h^k.

Purpose: a self-learner sees ⟨g⟩ and the group it is isomorphic to side by side. Turning one ring or pointing at a bead shows the correspondence on the other at once.

Output:
- `Cyclic Groups/cyclic-groups.html`: the right half, its pane mode, the sync and the linking.
- `assets/i18n/cyclic-groups.js`: one English-only key, replacing the key 122f203 added.
- A Playwright probe that proves every context end to end.

Architecture (the orchestrator's recommendation, adopted):
- The page keeps one singleton `state`, so the right half is an iframe of the page itself in a new pane mode (`?ringwin=1&isopane=1&isogen=h`). Pane mode is ringwin mode plus a flag, so it inherits everything ringwin already does:
  - the Ring-window body layout (enterRingWin);
  - no persistence and no shared-group writes;
  - the look (theme/lang) and state-report messages.
- The host never touches the pane's DOM, only postMessage, because file:// documents are opaque origins. It works the same whether the host is the top page (normal or full screen) or itself an extra Ring window's frame. In that case the pane is a nested iframe, and the messages go between that frame and its own child.

Discretion choices (made here; the executor follows them):
- One new string only. The right half's head reuses existing keys:
  - the group name from `cyclicGroups.fsRingCyclic` / `fsRingNotCyclic` / `fsRing`, the same names Ring windows carry;

  `iso.open` stays in all 31 blocks although the page no longer references it (i18n-check has no unused-key check). Note it in the SUMMARY for the catch-up pass.
- The pane also keeps per-group items that are on neither list, since D-01 says to start with everything per-group:
  - the coset explorer;
  - the identity note;
  - the auto-rotate speed ruler;
  - the arrow key (combine-key). Its drag-to-swap relays to the host, because swapping is shared.

  The pane's colour key (#legend-panel) shows its own group's colours while the host's Show key is on, with its identity-colour picker hidden. The pane's own Isomorphisms row and Splitview button are hidden: a pane never opens a pane, and the host's Splitview drives both.
- The pane's right-click menu relays only its layer items to the host. Its Export item downloads the pane's own ring, and its structure line shows the pane's group. The host's menu and Export act on the left ring, as before.
- On the normal page the host has no Splitview (that button exists only in Ring windows), so the pane shows no list there. The host's list stays under the frame.
- The host leads. Its render() re-sends its top bead (deduped), which covers a new g, a new arrangement or a direction flip. The pane sends its top only from turning paths. This avoids the two rings racing.
- Linking pauses while the orders differ. Every top/hover message carries `order`, and the receiver ignores it unless that equals its own link order: the host's |⟨g⟩|, or the pane's |G| while isogen still generates it. The right half stays open but unlinked.
- The pane query drops rot, top, autorotate and autospeed, so the pane opens still and is turned by the host's first top message. A chip click always (re)loads its group, even the one already shown.
- The right half is in memory only, and is gone after a page reload. It survives full-screen enter and exit by reloading from its latest reported query.
- Two adjacent pre-existing defects are fixed, each recorded in the SUMMARY as a Rule-1 deviation:
  - (a) The ring's right-click menu never showed inside any ringwin frame: `:root.is-ringwin .diagram-frame > :not(.ringwin-body)` hid it. The pane needs it, and extra Ring windows gain it too.
  - (b) In a frame, isoHref copied ringwin=1 into the chip's href, so a modified click opened a stripped page in the new tab.
