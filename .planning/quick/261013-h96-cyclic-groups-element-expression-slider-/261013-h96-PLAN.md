# Cyclic Groups: the Element expression becomes a slider by the ring

## Context
In `Cyclic Groups/cyclic-groups.html`, the full-screen tool, the ARRANGEMENT row holds three controls:
- `#arrange-select`: Sequential / Order by generator powers;
- `#arrange-gen-select`: the Generator, only in powers order;
- `#expr-select` in `#expr-pair`: the Element expression, i.e. Natural or powers of each generator. `syncExpr(G)` fills it and `state.expr` holds the choice.

The user wants the **Element expression** turned into a slider in the ring's area. Decided with the user:
- Only Element expression moves. Arrangement and Generator stay in the ARRANGEMENT row, and `#expr-pair` leaves that row.
- Only `Cyclic Groups/cyclic-groups.html` changes. `_Cyclic Groups` keeps its dropdown.
- **Placement:**
  - **Default:** a horizontal slider under the ring, above the rotation/ruler row. This holds with one group and with two groups open (the iso pane is an iframe of the same page, so it has its own slider under its own ring).
  - **Two groups open + Numerical view open:** each ring's slider stands **vertical**, with Natural at the top. The left (holding) ring's slider sits on the **left** of its ring, and the pane's on the **right** of its ring. A ring counts as "Numerical open" when its own `splitOn` is true: a linked pane follows the holder's split, and an unlinked one has its own.
    - Holding ring: side layout when `isoPane` is open (pane shown) and `splitOn`.
    - Pane: side layout when `IS_ISOPANE` and `splitOn`.
  - With one group and Numerical open, it stays under the ring.

## Task 1
- **Markup:** a slider block in the ring body (a new grid area, e.g. `expr`, between `ring` and the rotation row; the side layout puts it in a column beside `ring`). It holds:
  - a label using the existing `cyclicGroups.exprLabel` key ("Element expression");
  - `<input type="range" id="expr-slider">` with min 0, max = number of generators, step 1. Stop 0 is Natural; stop i is `G.generators[i-1]`.
  - a row or column of stop labels: "Natural" (`cyclicGroups.exprNatural`), then each generator rendered as `g^k` or just the generator number;
  - the current choice in full words (`cyclicGroups.arrangePowers` with `{g}`), also as `aria-valuetext`.

  Hidden for non-cyclic groups, as `#expr-pair` is now.
- **Behaviour:** sliding sets `state.expr` exactly as the select's change handler does today: same re-render, URL mirroring and iso messaging. Find the `exprSelectEl` change listener and reuse its body. Either keep `exprSelectEl` as a hidden source of truth synced to the slider, or remove it and drive everything from the slider; choose whichever keeps the URL/iso paths intact. `syncExpr(G)` also syncs the slider (max, labels, value). The ring's right-click menu, if it offers element expression, stays in sync.
- **Styling:**
  - palette tokens only;
  - site.css already keeps range inputs LTR;
  - the vertical mode uses `writing-mode: vertical-lr` with `direction: rtl` or equivalent, so Natural is at the top, and is keyboard-accessible (`aria-orientation`).
  - The `:root[dir="rtl"]` rules keep the slider and its numeric labels LTR.
- **Layout updates:** add the new area to the ring body's grid templates. The `.fs-win-body.is-ring` grid-template-areas variants in the page CSS include the iso pane, split, `is-sub-merged`, `is-iso-unlinked`, two-keys and one-legend variants. Make sure:
  - the ring still gets the spare height;
  - `keepLockClear()` (the padlock + shared Numerical button centred on the ring's middle) still works.
- **Strings:** reuse existing keys. Any new string goes in the en block of `assets/i18n/cyclic-groups.js` only and is listed in its header comment's English-first list.
- **Commit:** `feat(cyclic-groups): the element expression is a slider by the ring`.

## Verification
Headless Chrome (scratchpad `cdp.js`; click `#cg-enter` first):
- **One group:**
  - the slider is under the ring;
  - moving it to the stop for generator 5 renders the bead labels as powers of 5, as the select used to, and changes the URL;
  - back to Natural restores the labels.
- **Two groups** (open an iso chip), Numerical closed: each ring has a horizontal slider under it.
- **Numerical opened:**
  - the left slider is vertical on the left of the left ring;
  - the pane's slider is vertical on the right of its ring;
  - padlock and shared button placement are unchanged.
- **Padlock open, unlinked:** opening only the pane's Numerical puts only the pane's slider on the side.
- **Non-cyclic group** (multiplicative n=8): no slider.
- Keyboard arrows move the slider.
- **Regression scripts** (`g83wrap.js` wrapper with `isofix`, `unlink`, `rotsync`, `unlockopen`, `midbtn`) give the same results apart from position values that layout changes explain.
- **i18n checker:** `--header`, `--switcher-present` and `--coverage --all` show no new findings beyond the English-first ones.
- Zero console errors.
