# Quick 261013-h96: the Element expression is a slider by the ring

The Element expression is no longer a dropdown in the ARRANGEMENT row. It is now a slider in the ring's body. Stop 0 is Natural and stop i is the powers of `G.generators[i-1]`. Each stop is named by its generator, and the current choice is shown in full ("Powers of 5"), which is also the slider's `aria-valuetext`. Normally the slider sits under the ring. With two groups open and the Numerical view under them, each ring's slider stands upright on the ring's outer side, with Natural at the top.

## Changes (`Cyclic Groups/cyclic-groups.html` only)

- **Markup:**
  - `#expr-pair` and `#expr-select` were removed from the ARRANGEMENT row.
  - A new `#expr-slot` comes after `#ring-svg`, holding:
    - a label using `cyclicGroups.exprLabel`;
    - a "Natural" end word (`cyclicGroups.exprNatural`), which you can click;
    - `input#expr-slider` (range 0..number of generators, step 1);
    - the stop marks and their generator numbers;
    - an `<output>` showing the choice in full (`cyclicGroups.arrangePowers`).
  - `expr-slot` was added to `FS_PANELS` so both the page's ring body and the pane's ringwin body take it in.
  - Outside a ring body it stays `display:none`, so preview pages never show it.
- **JS:**
  - `syncExpr(G)` now fills the slider: its max, its value, the stops, the caption and `aria-valuetext`. It hides the slot for a group with no generator.
  - `setExprStop(i)` replaces the select's change handler. It sets `state.expr` and calls `render()`, which does the URL mirroring and the pane messaging. It runs on the slider's `input` event and when "Natural" is clicked.
  - `syncExprSide()` sets `.is-side` and `aria-orientation`:
    - holding ring: pane shown and its split open;
    - pane: `IS_ISOPANE && splitOn`.
  - `syncExprSide()` is called from `syncSplit()`, and a MutationObserver on `#iso-pane[hidden]` also calls it.
  - `thinExprStops()` names only every k-th stop when the stops are crowded (n=97 has 96 generators), and leaves the rest as bare ticks. A ResizeObserver re-runs it when the track changes size.
- **CSS:**
  - A new `expr` grid row between `ring` and the rotation/footer row, in every `.fs-win-body.is-ring` template: base, split, pane, pane+split, unlinked, two-keys, one-legend and both `is-sub-merged` variants.
  - The merged variants' row lists gained an `auto` row. In the split-based variants the old row-to-area mapping is kept exactly.
  - **Upright mode:** the slot shares the ring's own cell. It is `justify-self:start` on the holder and `end` in the pane. It uses `writing-mode:vertical-lr; direction:ltr`, so the minimum (Natural) is at the top. The ring svg gives up `--expr-side + 10px` of width on that side.
  - A size container hides the caption, then the label, when the ring's cell is very short.
  - Only palette tokens and `color-mix()` are used.
  - The thumb gets its own focus ring. The input's box shadow from `site.css` is dropped because it covered the stop labels.
  - `.expr-scale`, `#expr-slider` and `.expr-stops` are in the `:root[dir="rtl"]` LTR rule. The slider's words use `unicode-bidi:plaintext`, so Hebrew "חזקות של 5" reads correctly.
- **Strings:** no new keys; existing `exprLabel`, `exprNatural` and `arrangePowers` are reused.
- The ring's right-click menu offered no element expression, so it needed no change.

## Deviations from Plan

- **Slider is the only source of truth.** The plan allowed either option. The select is removed rather than kept hidden, and the URL and iso paths work unchanged because they read `state.expr`.
- **[Rule 2 - Accessibility/readability] Focus ring.** `site.css`'s `input:focus-visible` box shadow covered the stop labels. It is replaced on this slider by a ring around the thumb.
- **[Rule 1 - Layout] Short-ring fallback.** At small window heights (1000×720) the upright slot overflowed in the pane. A size container now hides the caption, then the label, below 190px and 140px.
