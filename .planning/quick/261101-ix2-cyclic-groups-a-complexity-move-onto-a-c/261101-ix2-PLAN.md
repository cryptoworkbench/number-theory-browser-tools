# Quick 261101-ix2: a complexity move onto a closed neither is refused

## Objective

The user, verbatim: "Make it such that when 'Neither' is selected in the form
preference, with Complexity to 'Complete', and a non-suitable logarithm base
for 'a2'; then setting the complexity slider to 'Compact' will not more the
form preference off of 'Neither'; instead the user will be notified through
flashes/animation that the combination of Complexity slider and Form
preference slider cannot be reached without first changing the logarithm base"

This replaces 261031-wef's J-01 fallback (a complexity change onto a closed
a/a2 took the remembered GCD/LCM form) with a refusal plus a flash.

## Decisions

- D-01 A complexity notch v is *blocked* when v > 0, v differs from the
  current complexity, the form slider shows neither (at JN: the remembered
  form) and the neither node at v (a2 at compact, a at complete) is closed
  under the current base. Symmetric for every such move.
- D-02 A blocked notch is drawn as it is (no is-closed: complexity notches stay
  unclosed), but its tooltip says the base must change first.
- D-03 Click, drag release, arrow keys and Home/End onto a blocked notch are
  refused: no state, no URL, no knob move; arrows do not skip over it.
- D-04 The refusal flashes: `is-flash-no` (lb-flash-no keyframes) on the
  refused complexity notch and on the form slider's neither notch; the boxes
  through `flashLogBases(G, node)`; `alarmLogLock('nudge')` when locked; an
  open map's `.fmap-stop[data-id=node]` with a new `fmap-flash-no` keyframe
  (role-warn twin of fmap-force-set). A rebuild during the flash carries it
  (negative animation-delay), as fslBand does.
- D-05 A double-click on a blocked notch calls `forceMapPosition(G, f, node,
  { cv, row })`: the complexity notch pulses with the box and the neither
  notch, the row comes from the memo when starting at JN, the lock alarms.
- D-06 settleMaps (a base change closing the selected neither) is unchanged.
