# Quick 261101-k8y: the Complexity and Form preference controls become real sliders

## Objective

The user, verbatim: "turn the complexity control into a actual slider with
three sloths [slots] and a knob which is always in one of the sloths. Same with
the 'Form preference' control."

Today each control in `formulaSliders(G, info)` reads as three labelled chips
with a framing knob. It becomes a slider: a horizontal track with three slots
(detents cut into it), a round knob always resting centred in one slot, and the
slot labels as small captions under their slots.

## Decisions

- D-01 Markup keeps `.fsl` (role="slider") and its three `.fsl-notch[data-v]`
  children, so state, tokens, URL keys, focus restore, the carry and every
  probe selector stay valid. A `.fsl-notch` is now a slot column holding a
  `.fsl-detent` (the hollow on the track) and a `.fsl-label` (its caption).
  Its text content is still the label.
- D-02 The knob is a round disc on the track, placed by CSS from `--v` at the
  slot centre (`100% / 6 * (2v + 1)`), so it is always centred in a slot when
  at rest. The drag clamps the knob between the outer slot centres.
- D-03 Drag release, click and keys commit through the unchanged `go(v)`.
  The snap and the click slide come from the existing carry (old `--v` / old
  inline `left`, then the new one) and the knob's `left` transition.
- D-04 A refused move (closed neither, blocked complexity) the knob "tries":
  from rest it nudges a few pixels toward the refused slot and springs back
  (Web Animations `transform`, skipped under reduced motion); from a drag it
  springs back by its transition. `style.left` and `--v` are untouched.
- D-05 Flash, force pulse and force-set classes stay on `.fsl-notch`; their
  animations move to the slot's detent (a glow ring) and label, inheriting the
  notch's inline `animation-delay` so the carry across rebuilds still works.
- D-06 Closed neither: the slot fades (opacity .4) with a dashed detent.
  Inert (JN) form slider: dimmed, grey knob. Palette tokens only.
- D-07 No new strings; i18n gates stay 31 / 4 / 109.
