<objective>
In Cyclic Groups' subgroup-to-group locked isomorphism, the holding ring's selected subgroup <g> is locked to the pane's whole group H. In that mode, merge the two bottom panels (the holder's Numerical list and the pane's own list) into ONE merged panel with the same structure, look and feel as the existing merged panel for whole-group locks. Every row of G stays on the left. A row whose generator lies in the locked subgroup S carries, on the right, the images of its members in their order, written {…} = <phi(g)>. Rows outside S have an empty right side. The user's example is "Cyclic Groups/subgroup_to_group_isomorphism".

Purpose: the user can read a subgroup-to-group isomorphism row by row, as the whole-group merge already allows, and browse G's other subgroups without the lock breaking.

Output: one commit on main touching only `Cyclic Groups/cyclic-groups.html`. Also produced, all untracked: the probe `merged-sub-check.mjs`, its `whole-baseline.json` and the SUMMARY.

Decisions:
- D-01 (LOCKED, from the orchestrator — do not revisit): row clicks in the merged panel keep the link on the locked subgroup <2>. A row inside S (for example <04>) selects <4> on the left ring and its image <2> on the pane's ring. A row outside S (for example <03>) selects on the left ring only, and the pane's ring shows no subgroup. The lock must not break when the user selects another row. This is implemented in Task 1 (pin + select message) and checked by the probe's `sub` scenario.
- Whole-group behaviour must stay unchanged byte for byte. Task 1 captures the baseline before any edit, and every task's verify compares against it.
- Claude's discretion, decided here and to be recorded in the SUMMARY:
  - DISC-1: the pin (isoPane.sub) persists through an unlock for as long as the pinned subgroup stays isomorphic to the pane's group, the way a whole link's isoPane.g pin persists. The open padlock therefore relocks onto the same <2>. The pin is dropped once the pinned subgroup stops being isomorphic to the pane's group, and the link then follows the subgroup on show again, as an unmerged subgroup link does today.
  - DISC-2: the sort option "Natural order on the right" (value 'r') is not offered in the subgroup case, because rows outside S have no right side. A stored state.subSortR is ignored there, not erased.
  - DISC-3: wherever the whole case writes phi(sort generator), the subgroup case writes phi(g^(|G|/|S|)), the generator of S that the sort generator gives. This applies to the pane's "Generator follows the sort", the sort options' "Exponent of g and h", and the gcd dividers' right line. Sorting G's rows by log_g orders S's rows by the exponent of that image, so the texts stay true.
  - DISC-4: a gcd divider carries its right (pane) half only when its rows lie in S, that is when the order of its rows divides |S|. Its d becomes |H| / order, which in the whole case is the same number as before.
  - DISC-5: a non-cyclic holder group (such as (Z/8Z)*) with a cyclic subgroup locked merges as well. Its right-hand expression row (H's selectors) is filled even though G's own expression and sort controls stay hidden.
  - DISC-6: the pane side's export (context menu and export window) lists only S's rows. Its lines stay index-aligned with the left list, with null for rows outside S.
</objective>
