# Quick task 261109-reel

## Task
Curve mode, modulus field double-clicked (rings not locked): an overlay with two big drum reels,
each holding 1 and the primes 5 … 97 (the moduli a curve takes here are a prime from 5 to 97 or a
product of two different ones up to 97; 1 on a reel lets the other's prime stand alone). A reel
turns by dragging (with inertia, snapping to an item on the payline), by the scroll wheel (one
item per notch) or by the arrow keys. The product of the two primes on the line shows as they
turn, flagged when it is no modulus a curve takes. Closing (button, Enter, Escape, a click
outside) puts a valid product in the modulus field and applies it as typing would.
