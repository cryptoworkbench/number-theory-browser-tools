# Quick task 261109-rtl

## Task
Beside the existing left-to-right ladder (top bit first), offer right to left: k as a sum of
powers of two, a¹, a², a⁴, … by repeated squaring, then the powers whose bit is 1 combined
(a⁸ ∗ a⁴ ∗ a¹). Both as radio items under Square and multiply in the formula lines' menu;
choosing one switches the mode on. State combineSqmWay ('ltr' default), URL combinesqmway=rtl,
shared with the pane.
