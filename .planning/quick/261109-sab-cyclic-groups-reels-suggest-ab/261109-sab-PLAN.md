# Quick task 261109-sab

## Task
When the reels' product is a modulus a curve takes but y² = x³ + ax + b is singular mod one of its
primes, find the nearest coefficients (fewest steps of a and b, within 0 … EC_COEF_MAX) that are
not, say so in the note, and on closing apply the modulus with them (fields a and b updated).
