# Quick task 261109-sq4: aᵏ in square-and-multiply form

## Task
While aᵏ (scalar combine) is on, a Square and multiply toggle (beside the k field, and in the
right-click menu of the formula lines) switches the formulas under the ring to the
square-and-multiply ladder: the exponent (reduced mod |G| as before) in binary, then one line
per bit from the top bit down, a^(2p+d) ≡ (a^p)² ∗ a^d worked out with numbers, the last line
ending at A in green. Additive groups and curves read the same with their own operation.

- Cyclic Groups/cyclic-groups.html: state.combineSqm (URL combinesqm=1, shared with the pane),
  toggle button, sqmRows(), combineExtraLines, unfoldShown, contextmenu, column groups.
- assets/i18n/cyclic-groups.js: combineSqm, combineSqmTitle (English first).
