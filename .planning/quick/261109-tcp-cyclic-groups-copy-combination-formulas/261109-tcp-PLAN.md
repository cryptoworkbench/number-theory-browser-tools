# Quick task 261109-tcp

## Task
Make the lines under the ring (a ∗ b, aᵏ, square and multiply) selectable and copyable: a press
on them (gaps between terms included) selects text instead of turning the ring; a copy gives
each touched line in plain text (raised parts as ^(…), lowered as _(…)); the right-click menu on
the lines gets Copy these formulas (all lines).
