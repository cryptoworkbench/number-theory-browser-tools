# 261115-kr1: Finite Groups congruence wheel — Keep the numbers readable at every turn

Add a switch under Show the congruence wheel (ring menu's Congruence wheel submenu), off by default. Off: the wheel is drawn with the identity at the top and rotated to where the identity is; its cells, rings and numbers are reused across turns rather than rebuilt, numbers not re-flipped. On: today's behaviour. Persisted (cyclic-groups-ring-wheel-upright), link param wheelupright. English only.
