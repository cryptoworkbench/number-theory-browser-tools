Chinese Remainder Theorem

Each congruence on its own picks out an evenly-spaced family of numbers — every third number, every fifth number, and so on. When the moduli share no common factor, those families cross at exactly one place in every stretch of lcm numbers. That single crossing is the simultaneous solution every row agrees on.
