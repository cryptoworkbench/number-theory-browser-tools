Fermat's Method

Fermat's method: every odd number N can be written as a difference of two squares, N = a² − b². Find the right b² to "complete the square" of N, and the factorization (a−b)(a+b) = N falls out — with a picture to prove it.

N + b² = a² N = a² − b² N = (a − b)(a + b) We search a = ⌈√N⌉, ⌈√N⌉+1, … until a² − N is itself a perfect square b².
