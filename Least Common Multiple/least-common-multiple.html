Least Common Multiple

Stack squares of side a in one tower and squares of side b in another, always adding the next square to whichever tower is shorter. The first height both towers reach together is the least common multiple of a and b — and the least common denominator of any two fractions over a and b.

The towers meet at a·b / gcd(a, b) — watch that gcd come out of the Euclidean algorithm →
