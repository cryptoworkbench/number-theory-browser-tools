Prime Venn Diagram

The two circles are two numbers, each written out as its prime factorization — everything inside a circle multiplies to that number. The primes in the overlap are the ones both numbers share, so the overlap multiplies to their GCD.

The three circles are three numbers, each written out as its prime factorization — everything inside a circle multiplies to that number. A prime sitting where two circles overlap is shared by exactly those two, so that whole lens multiplies to their GCD. A prime sitting where all three circles overlap is shared by all three, so the centre multiplies to the GCD of all three numbers.
